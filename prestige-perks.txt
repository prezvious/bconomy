# Prestige Perks

## Investiture
Reduces how much it costs to ascend

## Cronyism
Lowers the price of ranking up

## Backchannel
Reduces the percentage fee taken on market trades

## Partiality
Improves the odds of landing a work bonus

## Serendipity
Increases the multiplier applied to rare item finds

## Numismatist
Raises the maximum bet allowed on a coinflip

## Amnesiac
Improves the odds that an action's cooldown gets skipped when triggered
