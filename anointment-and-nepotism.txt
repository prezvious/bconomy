# Investiture and Cronyism

Prestige Cost = Base Rank Cost × (P + 1)

Discount (%) = 2.5 × Level

Final Cost = Base Cost × (1 − 0.025 × Level)

Total Prestige Cost = Sum of all 107 rank costs (after Cronyism discount) + Ascension cost (after Investiture discount)

Since Cronyism affects every rank-up, its discount is applied 107 times during one prestige. Investiture is only applied once, when you ascend.

Progression:
1. Rank 1 -> Rank 107 (each rank discounted by Cronyism)
2. Ascend (discounted by Investiture)
3. Prestige count increases by 1
4. Repeat
